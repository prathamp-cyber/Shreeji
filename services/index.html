Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying or selling commercial land in Kutch.

What is NA land in Gujarat and why is it important for industrial projects?

NA stands for Non-Agricultural land. In Gujarat, agricultural land cannot be used for commercial or industrial purposes without obtaining NA permission from the Collectorate. Buying pre-approved NA land saves significant time and legal paperwork.

Why is Kutch considered a top investment hub for commercial logistics?

Kutch houses India's premier deep-water ports (Kandla and Mundra), direct rail links, national highways, and the world's largest hybrid renewable energy park in Khavda, making it an unprecedented strategic corridor for logistics and manufacturing.

How do you assist with due diligence and title verification?

Our legal team scrutinizes 30-year revenue search reports, 7/12 extracts, encumbrance certificates, zoning compliance, and boundary maps to ensure 100% clean title transfer before any transaction takes place.

What sizes of land plots are available through your consultancy?

We handle land parcels ranging from small GIDC plots (5,000 sq meters) up to massive industrial land banks exceeding 200+ acres suitable for mega solar parks and logistics parks.
