Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers regarding commercial rental terms, lease agreements, security deposits, location choices, and landlord listing in Gandhidham & Adipur.

Why is Gandhidham & Adipur considered the top commercial hub in Kutch?

Gandhidham and Adipur serve as the commercial capital of Kutch due to their proximity to Deendayal Port (Kandla) and Mundra SEZ. The region features major banking networks, shipping company headquarters, high footfall retail markets (Tagor Road, Sector 12), and direct highway connectivity via NH 41.

Which commercial areas offer the highest footfall for retail shops?

Tagor Road and Sector 12 in Gandhidham are the premier high-footfall corridors ideal for retail showrooms, brand outlets, banks, and restaurants. In Adipur, the Main Market and Station Road area offer excellent visibility for retail businesses and coaching centers.

What are the standard security deposit requirements for renting a commercial space?

Typically, commercial properties in Gandhidham & Adipur require an advance security deposit equivalent to 3 to 6 months of rent, depending on the property type, location, and length of lease agreement. The deposit is fully refundable at the end of tenancy as per lease terms.

Can I customize or renovate the leased commercial office/shop space?

Yes. Non-structural interior modifications (such as glass partitions, false ceilings, flooring, painting, and AC ducting) are permitted with prior written approval from the landlord. Most landlords also offer a rent-free fit-out period (15 to 45 days) for interior setup.

What is the standard commercial lease agreement duration?

Standard commercial lease agreements are structured for 11 months (renewable), 3 years, 5 years, or up to 9 years for large corporate offices, showrooms, and commercial land plots, usually with a 1-year to 3-year lock-in period.

How do you screen and verify prospective commercial tenants for my property?

We conduct background checks including company registration/GST audit, corporate track record, financial stability evaluation, and previous tenancy references before presenting tenant profiles to landlords.

Is a registered lease agreement mandatory for commercial properties?

Under Indian law, lease agreements exceeding 11 months must be legally registered with the Sub-Registrar Office with proper stamp duty payment. For 11-month leases, a notarized commercial rent agreement is standard.

Who pays GST and municipal property taxes in a commercial lease?

Commercial rent attracts 18% GST if the landlord is GST-registered or under RCM for corporate tenants. Property taxes are generally paid by the property owner, unless specifically agreed upon in the lease contract.

How does rent escalation work in commercial leases?

A standard rent escalation clause specifies an annual or 3-yearly increase of 5% to 15% on the prevailing monthly rent, ensuring property yield keeps pace with market inflation.
