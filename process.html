Transparent & Structured Workflow

Our Seamless 5-Step Commercial Process

Whether you are looking to rent a commercial shop, lease office spaces, or list your commercial property, our process ensures total legal compliance and speed.

For Tenants & Businesses

The Commercial Tenant Journey

How we help you find, inspect, negotiate, and legally secure your commercial space in Gandhidham & Adipur.

1

Requirement Analysis

We capture your business specifications: required sq.ft area, preferred location (Tagor Road, Sector 12, Adipur), budget limit, footfall needs, and power requirements.

2

Curated Shortlisting

Our specialists filter our database of verified commercial properties and send you handpicked options matching your precise commercial criteria.

3

Guided Site Inspections

We arrange guided physical site tours, evaluating space layouts, frontage visibility, ceiling height, customer parking, and structural suitability.

4

Terms Negotiation

We negotiate monthly rent, security deposit terms, rent escalation percentage, lock-in period, and fit-out rent-free period with the property owner.

5

Lease Signing & Handover

We draft legally binding lease agreements, complete stamp duty registration, perform meter readings, and hand over property keys seamlessly.

For Property Owners

The Landlord Property Listing Journey

How we market your commercial property, verify prospective corporate tenants, and ensure maximum rental returns.

1

Property Valuation

Our team visits your shop, office, or commercial plot to evaluate fair market rental rates and suggest high-yield presentation tips.

2

Strategic Marketing

We list your property across our investor network, digital channels, and direct corporate outreach to reach active business seekers.

3

Tenant Screening

We conduct background checks, GST verification, and financial stability audits on prospective corporate or retail tenants.

4

Lease Framing

We structure robust lease terms incorporating standard annual escalation (e.g. 5-10%), security deposit safeguards, and maintenance responsibilities.

5

Possession & Setup

We collect the security deposit and advance rent on your behalf, complete key handover, and set up smooth monthly rental transfers.

Compliance & Documentation

Required Documents Checklist

Keep these documents ready to accelerate your commercial transaction process.

For Business Tenants

  • PAN Card of Business Entity / Proprietor
  • Aadhaar Card & Passport Size Photographs
  • GST Registration Certificate
  • Shop & Establishment / Trade License copy
  • Advance Security Deposit & Initial Month Rent

For Property Owners

  • Original Property Title Deed / Allotment Letter
  • Recent Municipal Property Tax Payment Receipts
  • Electricity Meter Connection & Latest Bill
  • Owner PAN & Aadhaar Identification Proof
  • Bank Details for Monthly Rent Direct Credit
